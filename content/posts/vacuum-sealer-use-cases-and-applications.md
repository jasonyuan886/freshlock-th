## Meal Prep & Batch Cooking

Meal prep has become a lifestyle for millions of busy professionals, parents, and fitness enthusiasts. But here's the problem: most meal-prepped food lasts only 2–3 days in the refrigerator before quality degrades. Vacuum sealing changes that equation entirely.

### Why Vacuum Sealing Is Essential for Meal Prep

When you remove air from food storage bags, you dramatically slow down oxidation and bacterial growth. Vacuum-sealed meal prep containers can stay fresh for **5–7 days in the refrigerator** — more than double the shelf life of standard storage methods. For freezer storage, vacuum-sealed meals can last **2–3 years** compared to 6–12 months with conventional wrapping.

| Storage Method | Fridge Shelf Life | Freezer Shelf Life |
|---|---|---|
| Standard container | 2–3 days | 6–12 months |
| Plastic wrap | 2–3 days | 6–12 months |
| Vacuum-sealed bag | 5–7 days | 2–3 years |

### The Meal Prep Workflow

1. **Batch cook** your proteins, grains, and vegetables on Sunday
2. **Portion** meals into individual serving sizes
3. **Vacuum seal** each portion using FreshLock Pro's -60kPa suction
4. **Store** in the refrigerator (for the week ahead) or freezer (for longer-term)
5. **Reheat** when ready to eat — vacuum-sealed bags can often go straight into a water bath

### Best Foods for Vacuum-Sealed Meal Prep

- **Cooked meats**: Chicken breast, ground beef, pulled pork, meatballs
- **Grains**: Rice, quinoa, pasta (cook ahead, vacuum seal, reheat in minutes)

- **Vegetables**: Blanched veggies retain color and nutrients when vacuum sealed

## Cooking Techniques

Vacuum sealing isn't just for storage — it's a foundational technique for several advanced cooking methods. Professional chefs have long relied on vacuum sealing, and now home cooks can achieve similar results with an affordable handheld sealer.

### Sous Vide

Sous vide cooking — cooking food in a temperature-controlled water bath — requires food to be sealed in a bag to prevent water from diluting flavors. But **can you use FreshLock Pro valve bags for sous vide?**

**Yes — with important conditions:**

| Condition | Details |
|---|---|
| Temperature | FreshLock valve bags are safe for sous vide cooking at temperatures **up to 80°C (176°F)** |
| Cooking duration | Best for sessions under 4 hours; extended low-temp cooks may compromise the valve |
| Food type | Ideal for steaks, chicken breasts, fish fillets, vegetables, and eggs |
| Not recommended for | Extended braises (24+ hours), very high-temperature water baths |

Since FreshLock Pro uses a **no thermal seal** valve system (no heating element is used to seal the bag), the valve must remain above the water line during sous vide cooking. This is a simple technique: clip the top of the bag to the side of your container so the valve stays dry.

### Marinating

Vacuum sealing dramatically accelerates the marinating process. In a vacuum environment, the absence of air pressure causes the food's pores to open, allowing marinades to penetrate **3–5x faster** than traditional methods.

- **Traditional marinating**: 4–24 hours for deep flavor penetration
- **Vacuum marinating**: 20–60 minutes for similar results

Simply place your meat or vegetables in a FreshLock valve bag with your marinade, vacuum-seal, and refrigerate. The vacuum environment forces the marinade into the food's cellular structure, delivering deeper flavor in a fraction of the time.
